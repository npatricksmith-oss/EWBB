### Key Mismatches: Stretch (Ranked by Visual Impact)

1. **Entire Pre-Pitch & Set Stance is Square (0.000–0.190)**
   - *Game:* Chest and both feet face squarely toward home plate while coming set.
   - *Reference (Wacha / Darvish):* Pitcher is fully side-on from the start; right foot rests parallel against the rubber, chest faces 3B (viewer’s left), head turned toward plate.
   - *Fix:* Rotate entire body 90° side-on (chest facing left, right foot planted sideways) before hands come together at the belt.

2. **Leg Lift Initiates Square, Snaps Sideways Late (0.230–0.280)**
   - *Game:* At 0.230, the front knee raises straight toward the camera with the torso square; at 0.280, the body abruptly snaps side-on.
   - *Reference:* Leg lift begins and remains entirely in the side-on plane; front knee lifts toward chest without exposing the torso.
   - *Fix:* Remove the front-facing knee lift at 0.230; begin leg lift directly from the side-on set stance.

### Key Mismatches: Windup (Ranked by Visual Impact)

1. **Weak Knee Lift & Late Torso Coil (0.230–0.300)**
   - *Game:* At 0.270, the lift knee barely reaches belt height with a flat side-on torso. At 0.300, jersey number "34" pops in abruptly.
   - *Reference (Ryan 2.5–3.5s / Darvish 01.037s):* Ryan’s signature high kick pulls the knee tight to chest/chin level, coiling past 90° so his back and jersey number show throughout peak balance.
   - *Fix:* Raise the knee from belt to upper chest height, angle the thigh upward 45°, and rotate the torso so the back faces the viewer during peak lift (0.270–0.300).

2. **Missing Pivot Foot Turn on Rocker Step (0.030–0.170)**
   - *Game:* Feet remain planted facing home plate throughout the overhead hand pump.
   - *Reference (Ryan 0.67–2.17s):* As hands rise, left foot steps back into rocker step, and right pivot foot shifts parallel to the rubber.
   - *Fix:* Turn the right pivot foot 90° sideways on the rubber as hands go overhead.

3. **Stiff Arm Break / Stride Disconnect (0.330–0.360)**
   - *Game:* Glove arm thrusts out stiffly toward 3B while throwing hand hangs passively before suddenly jumping overhead at 0.360.
   - *Reference:* Hands break smoothly together at chest height; throwing arm moves down and back into scapular load as the front leg uncoils toward home plate.
   - *Fix:* Separate hands at chest level; show the throwing arm dropping back behind the hip while striding.
