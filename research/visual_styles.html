⚾ Earl Weaver Baseball — Visual Styles Comparison

Original archival screenshots from research/images/ paired with live HTML5 Canvas mockups of the 3 visual options.

Style A: 1987 Amiga EWB 1.0 Classic

Classic Split-Screen Pixel Art

Faithful recreation of Eddie Dombrower's signature 1987 Amiga layout: 2/3 isometric diamond on the left + 1/3 behind-the-plate pitcher/batter duel on the right.

  • Pros: Maximum 1987 nostalgia; shows baserunners & pitch break simultaneously.
  • Look: 32-color Amiga palette, chunky pixel fonts, scoreboard ticker at bottom.
Style B: 1991 EWB II VGA Broadcast

EWB II TV-Camera + Multi-Deck Stadium

The 1991 256-color VGA sequel look: close-up over-the-shoulder pitcher/batter view that cuts to a zoomed-out multi-deck stadium when the ball is hit.

  • Pros: Larger batter/pitcher sprites; detailed upper-deck stadium backdrops.
  • Cons: Loses the simultaneous diamond view during the pitch (harder to watch base stealers).
★ Recommended — Style C: Modern Retro-Pro Hybrid

Crisp High-DPI 1987 Split-Screen + Modern Dashboard

Combines the beloved 1987 Amiga split-screen field + Fenway/Wrigley custom ballpark walls with razor-sharp box scores, pitch-by-pitch log, and "Ask Earl" strategy cards.

  • Best of Both Worlds: Preserves Dombrower's iconic split-screen diamond + batter box while giving you crisp, readable stat tables, live win-probability/platoon splits, and smooth 60fps ball physics & shadows.
  • Why TypeScript + HTML5 Canvas wins here: Canvas renders the 2D ballpark, wall heights, ball flight shadow, and pixel/vector players at 60fps, while HTML/CSS renders ultra-clean Commissioner's Disk stat tables and "Ask Earl" dialogs.
